# Sample data

`simulation_results.csv` holds the per-run metrics behind every result in the notebook: 2 protocols × 11 cooperator shares × 30 seeds = 660 one-hour simulations. The data are synthetic and produced by the notebook's simulator.

Columns: participation, protocol, seed, mean_delay, p95_delay, value_weighted_delay, coop_delay, defector_delay, throughput_per_min, completion, conflicts_per_hour.
